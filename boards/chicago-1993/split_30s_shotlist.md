## Matched cuts across the seam
- 03.50: the puddle rain falls down as the ladle pours down.
- 08.00: the bus R→L as the Acroni slab R→L (the hiss, then the roar from about 8.0).
- 13.21: ic92 cars R→L as the 1938 hot bar R→L (orange).
- 15.42: skyline L→R as the clouds L→R.
- 19.00: both panels hit P4–5 (L canyon | Acroni fast sweep).
- 21.33/21.38: Amtrak window R→L as the Inland mill window R→L.
- 22.25/22.29: both cut together; the shelter freezes at about 24.0 while the orange pour runs on to 30.0.

## Audio
Rain bed (t-rain-street crossfaded into t-rain-pouring), a synthesized air-brake hiss at 8.0, and a synthesized brown-noise mill roar from 8.0 to 22.3. Counter-world source audio is mixed under at a low level, for the Acroni rolls, Acroni fast and Inland train only. The narrated clips (NOAA, Koverhar, Steel: Man's Servant) are muted after a Whisper speech check found narration in them. No music. -18 LUFS.
